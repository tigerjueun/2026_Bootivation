Key validated behaviors
-----------------------
- Exact customer payment clears without a theft alert.
- No payment and partial payment produce critical classifications.
- Rider progress and tray mismatch calculations remain stable.
- Final Vision POS and EXIT messages require the `state` field and `retail ` prefix.
- Non-final Vision states are rejected by the external adapter.
- A cumulative Vision delta such as `A: 0 → 2` becomes one `REMOVE_CANDIDATE` event with `qty=2`, avoiding loss through duplicate-detection cooldown.
- Customer POS detailed summary does not double-count `PAY_DONE`.
- Vision physical removal and Rider POS verification remain separate ledgers.
- GREEN requires `order == rider_removed == rider_checked_items`.
- Wrong physical or POS-confirmed items result in RED.
- POS session reset rolls back the check ledger without restoring already removed physical inventory.
- Legacy customer POS strings remain parseable.
